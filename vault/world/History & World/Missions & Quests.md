title: "Missions & Quests"
---
- ### Bees Make Honey
	- *The world is funny. Bees make honey.*
	- Introductory quest- exploring the world of the faction you're in

- ### Time Splitter
	- A quest to teach about zone splitting
	- A Cleric requests a relic for his experiments, but they're hard to come by these days.
	- *Loot*: 1 Relic, unlock ability to [[Zone Splitting|Zone Split]]

- ### False Prophet
	- The pastor tells you about a distraught follower that went out into the wild claiming he was a prophet, but he is no chosen one. He is but a false prophet corrupted

- ### Strange Sunday
	- Somethings gone wrong. Things are not where they're supposed to be. You're finding artifacts splitting through the world.
