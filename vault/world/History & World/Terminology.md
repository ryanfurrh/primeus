#### Latin
----
**Words**
- Animus - vital force or spirit that gives life to a living being
- Anticus, antica anticæ
- Flux (Fluxus) -  The act of flowing; a continuous moving on or passing by, as of a flowing stream
- Modus
- Primae -
- Primaevus (prīmus = "first" & aevum = "age") - The ancient ones
	- in classical Latin  it's typically used as an adjective i.e. "primaevus homo" - ancient man / primeval man
- Reliquia Divina - Relic of the divine
- Tempus -
- Prima Materia

**Phrases**
- E Plurbis Unum - out of many one
- Novus ōrdō sēclōrum -  new order of the ages
- Principium conscientiae - Principle or beginning of consciousness
- Origines mentis - Origins of the mind
- tempus fugit - time flies
