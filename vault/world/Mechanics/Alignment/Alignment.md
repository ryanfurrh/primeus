title: "Alignment"
---
### #Natural
---
-  Associated with the religious relationship with the flux. Aligned with holistic values, divinity, oneness with the natural world, and modesty
	- #Harmonious - one with nature and the natural world, community and family values.
		- *Desire* - personal and immediate communal prosperity
	- #Egotistic - Feelings of grandeur and power. Personal righteousness, austerity, and self-actuation
		- *Desire* - to harness the power of the gods

### #Technical
---
- The governmental ties to the flux as a natural resource of energy. Related to technological development, academic research, productivity, and advanced weaponry
	- #Virtuous/Altruistic - Improve the wellbeing of humanity through technological innovation.
		- *Desire* - to alter our existence for the good of the collective
	- #Exploitative - Individualism and stoicism. Wealth, status, and resource stockpiling
		- *Desire* - to control and conquer. power and wealth.
