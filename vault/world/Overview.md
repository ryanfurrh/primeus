### Narrative
----
##### The Great Awakening
- Some say it was the moment the atom was first split, that alerted us to the presence of flux energy in our world. An opening of Pandora's Box.
	- For over a decade after this event, the DOE and the DOD methodically researched these signals, stumbling their way into a form of control over flux energy.
- Some believe the great awakening came as a broadcast from the Primaevus to our collective consciousness.

At that time it was only coveted by a small group of devotees; individuals with seemingly no ties to each other coming from all corners of the world. They each found themselves overcome by a strange calling, a beckoning, to come forth. Like missionaries on their pilgrimage to Mecca. The Order was born.

The Sacred Lands were tended to in a quiet peace for decades. The once small group of strangers had quickly become a thriving congregation of devout evangelists.

In the early 1980s, in the face of an alarming energy crisis a small team of researchers contracted by the US government were tasked with searching for alternative energy sources.
